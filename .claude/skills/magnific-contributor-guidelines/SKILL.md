# Magnific Contributors guidelines

Source: the official "Magnific Contributors" slide deck, read from a screen recording the user made (October 2026). Everything below is from the deck itself, not from blogs. Royalty percentages and payout terms are NOT in the deck, so say they are unconfirmed rather than quoting a number.

Freepik now operates as Magnific. Treat both names as the same platform.

## How to use this skill

1. Work out what the user has: a photo, an EPS vector, a PSD (mockup, graphic element, template), an AI-generated file, a metadata/CSV question, or a rejection to diagnose.
2. Check it against the matching section below and the checklist at the end.
3. Report pass/fail per rule, give the exact limit, and explain why the rule exists (reviewers reject for it). Name the slide topic so the user can find it in the deck.
4. If the question touches something not covered here, check `references/slide-log.md` (what has and has not been read) and say plainly that the deck section was not read yet, instead of guessing.

## Accepted content and file specs

| Type | Format | Dimensions | Size | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photo | JPG | 2,000–10,000 px; 4–100 MP | 1.5–80 MB | RGB; sRGB, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto RGB or Display P3; lowest compression |
| Vector | EPS (required) | none stated | 0.5–80 MB | RGB; same profiles |
| Vector preview | JPG, same file name as the EPS | 2,000–10,000 px | 1.5–80 MB | |
| PSD | PSD, Photoshop CC version | 1,000–5,000 px; 4–100 MP; must match standard paper sizes | 1.5–250 MB | RGB; same profiles |
| PSD preview | JPG, same file name | 2,000–10,000 px | 1.5–80 MB | |

Photos should be clear and sharp, with no watermarks, logos, or excessive editing. Do not upscale to reach the minimum size; upscaling adds artificial pixels and is rejected for low quality.

Vectors need clean paths, organised layers, no rasterised text or unnecessary gradients, and every element grouped and named. PSD files need layers named in English, not flattened, with all fonts free for commercial use and still editable.

Standard paper sizes the PSD slide lists: EU business card 85×55 mm, postcard 148×105 mm, A2 poster 420×594 mm, roll-up banner 850×2,000 mm, US tabloid 11×17 in, legal 8.5×14 in, US letter 8.5×11 in, half letter 5.5×8.5 in, US business card 3.5×2 in, invitation 5×7 in, RSVP card 5×3.5 in.

## Accounts, levels and limits

- **Level 1:** upload 150–200 assets for review; every file in the test submission must use the same format. Web upload only. Once the first assets are approved, collaboration starts.
- **Level 2:** upload 1–200 assets at a time, web upload only. 500 published assets are needed to reach Level 3.
- **Level 3:** upload 1–200 assets, FTP or web upload.
- **Monthly limit:** 200 files per month per account (resets at the start of the month).
- **Upload folder:** holds 200 files at a time; remove or send some for review to add more.
- **Review:** up to 25 working days. Statuses: under review, approved, rejected. Check "My files"; rejection reasons appear in the file's comments.
- **Releases:** identifiable people need a model release and identifiable property needs a property release.
- The contributor is responsible for authorship and for knowing Magnific's licences. Failing the legal points can mean blocked access or a terminated account.

## Metadata

- Keywords: 5–20 per file, all in English (proper nouns excepted), precise and relevant to subject, setting, colours, style, mood, emotions. Include variations and synonyms.
- Avoid: repetition, misleading campaign tags, exhaustive colour lists, file types (e.g. EPS10, JPG), spelling errors, numerals and dates unless needed (e.g. "4th of July"), special characters or symbols.
- Describe only what can be seen on the resource or its concept.
- Title example from the deck: `Promotional Poster "Pool Days Forever" with Summer Theme`.
- Two ways to add keywords: CSV upload to the panel, or manually in the contributor dashboard.
- **CSV format:** fields separated by semicolons (;), tags separated by commas (,). Columns: `File name; Title; Keywords; (Prompt, if AI-generated); Model`. Example: `"beautiful-sunset.jpg"; "Beautiful sunset"; "sunset,sun,summer,beach,mountain"; "Beautiful sunset on the beach in summer"; "Midjourney 5"`. Create it in Excel (save as CSV), Google Sheets (export CSV) or OpenOffice Calc (Text CSV); use semicolon as the field separator. Upload via FTP tool (e.g. FileZilla) or the panel's Upload section.

## What sells

Mix evergreen (education, health, business, emotions, nature), seasonal (Christmas, Back to School, Valentine's, Black Friday) and trend content. Buyers look for commercially useful editable content (posters, social templates, resumes), diversity (ages, body types, skin tones), clean organised files (layered PSDs, customizable vectors), clear concepts, text-friendly layouts with clean space, and original content. Variations of published work (colour changes, crops, added elements) do not count as original; assets must be created from scratch.

## AI-generated assets

- Accepted types: photos, vectors and PSD files.
- Tag every AI file with the "Created with artificial intelligence" button in Upload, or add `_ai_generated` in the CSV. The upload panel also has Prompt and Base model fields.
- Rejected: malformations (extra fingers, deformed bodies), flat or low-detail textures, repetitive or very similar images from the same prompt, and distorted or unreadable text (e.g. "Merry Clistmas").
- AI PSD cutouts must be accurately cropped with clean edges. AI PSD templates and mockups need individually selectable, accurately cropped elements and fully working smart objects.
- All PSD/EPS must be fully functional: layers organised and grouped, text/shapes/components fully editable, immediately usable with no clean-up.

## Vector rules

- **Live Trace** is allowed only for textures made with traditional media (watercolour, ink, pencil, charcoal) and only when it adds value that basic vector tools cannot. Never trace flat illustrations, logos or UI elements.
- When tracing is allowed: group related elements, remove stray anchors and paths, and do not flatten everything into one compound path. Use the "stacked" method (shapes layered on top, accurate but heavier) rather than "cutout" (puzzle pieces, can lose subtle detail).
- Composition: balanced layout, even distribution, consistent margins, clear separation between elements, visual hierarchy, consistent typography; keep packs tidy and away from the edges.
- Layers: group each element separately and name them in English.

## PSD rules

- **Mockups:** the editable area must show a realistic design or text so the mockup's function and look are clear. A flat colour or empty placeholder in that area is rejected (this includes a plain green screen). An editable background is recommended.
- **Graphic elements:** individually on transparent backgrounds, cleanly cut with no halos, leftover edges or background artefacts.
- **Masks on smart objects** must be linked to the object (exception: scene-creator PSDs may unlink them).

## Reasons for rejection

General: plagiarism (reusing others' elements, even modified), sensitive content (explicit violence, sexual content, politically offensive, drug use), similar or duplicate content (same file, minimal changes, small variation in composition, framing, colours or effects), outdated content (past dates such as "Happy New Year 2022" or a past-year calendar), incorrect dimensions or size (table above), error in the file (does not open or renders wrongly), quality standards (outdated, repetitive or poorly usable; must open and edit correctly), invalid or incomplete upload (not a valid EPS/JPG, missing preview or preview with a different name, corrupted, EPS missing), low quality preview (too few pixels, blurry), spelling and grammar, functionality (non-working smart objects, bitmaps embedded in EPS/PSD, files that need another program such as a vector smart object that needs Illustrator), image trace (detail lost in vectorising).

Bad practices: Magnific may suspend or cancel an account without notice, keep accumulated income, and pursue legal action to recover earnings.

Trademark and copyright: no logos or iconic designs even with the logo removed (examples: Polaroid frames, Volkswagen T1 van, LEGO blocks); no copyrighted landmarks or public-space items even if not the main subject (the slide shows Christ the Redeemer); no characters with recognisable traits of franchises (Disney, Pixar, Nickelodeon, Pokémon-like), even modified. Generic, up-to-date social media icons (TikTok, Instagram, X) are allowed.

Repeated submissions: do not upload series where only a number (e.g. "8th" vs "9th Anniversary") or a minimal colour changes. If the number or text is editable, extra versions are not allowed; for non-editable text designs a limited range, typically 1–10, is allowed if clearly useful.

## Additional rules (from the slow re-read)

**How files are judged:** each submission is reviewed by moderators for originality (compared with your own portfolio and the existing library), visual quality, commercial relevance (market trends and demand), file structure and usability (layer naming, grouping, editability for vectors and PSDs), accuracy of titles and keywords, legal compliance, and ethical suitability (nothing harmful, offensive or misleading).

**Unpublishing:** Published Content, select files, "Unpublish asset"; removal and de-indexing can take up to 24 hours; you can unpublish several at once and republish by uploading again. Published content cannot be edited; you can only add model releases or unpublish it.

**PSD technical details**
- Print files 300 ppi; screen/web content 150 ppi.
- ISO sizes: A3 297×420 mm, A4 210×297, A5 148×210, A6 105×148, DL 99×210. Web banners: leaderboard 728×90 px, medium rectangle 300×250, skyscraper 160×600, square 250×250, half page 300×600.
- Preview background for graphic elements: solid white or a checkered transparency pattern (shows no unintended background remains).
- Naming: smart-object group `Place your design here (Double click to edit)`, layer inside it `Place your design here`; colour adjustment layers `Color` or `Color [item]` (e.g. `Color T-shirt`); scene effects `FX [effect]` (FX light, FX shadow, FX vignette).
- Masks on smart objects stay linked (scene creators excepted). Smart objects must be fully functional and aligned so the final result looks realistic.
- **Templates (editorial designs):** no photographs may be embedded in PSD/EPS, not on any layer, not as a background, not inside a smart object. A sample photo may appear only in the JPG preview, must be free for commercial use, and the preview must show the label "IMAGE NOT INCLUDED"; a missing label is rejected as "MISSING IMAGE NOT INCLUDED". Templates built around a person-shaped cut-out silhouette are rejected because other images won't fit. (Mockups differ: their smart-object area must be working and show a realistic design.)

**Photo aesthetics (rejected if present):** chromatic aberration (green/magenta fringes; fix in post), chromatic noise or grain, excessive saturation or over-aggressive filters, accidental or unjustified blur (blur needs an artistic purpose), motion blur without intent, excessive sharpening (halos), underexposure, overexposure, flat lighting, excessive contrast, upscaled images, poor framing or cluttered composition with cropped or out-of-focus elements.

**Photo legal issues:** every recognisable person, primary or incidental, needs a signed model release; minors need a release signed by a parent or guardian; private property needs a property release (zoos and similar venues need permission); wild animals are fine if not harmed or endangered; domestic pets need an owner's property release. AI photos that show recognisable people or private locations need releases too, and realistic AI photos must meet all photo rules (correct anatomy and proportions, clean detail).

**AI vectors (slide 6):** all elements individually selectable, no Live Trace, no fragmented vectors, clear and consistent structure, and all vector standard guidelines apply.

**Typography:** type must be coherent with the design (mixed or clashing fonts are rejected) and lettering must be well-composed and consistent with the final composition.

**Vector rejection reasons:** loss of detail from image trace; elements left outside the artboard (workspace must be clean); a very simple design or single icon must be sold as part of a set; reuse of other authors' vector elements is plagiarism.

**PSD rejection reasons:** images included in the PSD (see above); bad cut (halos, leftover edges, background artefacts).

**Dates and devices:** any design with a year must show the current calendar year; near the end of the year you may submit the next year's designs. Devices shown must be recent, ideally no older than 1–2 years.

**Prohibited elements:** signatures, watermarks (including from phone cameras or AI tools) or any author identifiers; mentions of Magnific ("designed by Magnific"); QR codes or links to real websites or with personal data; people who haven't consented; individuals' names or addresses used to give the design meaning.

**Copyright details:** rejected if they include protected logos, brand names or characters; modified or recreated designs too close to registered works; elements by other authors even under CC0; designs "inspired by" someone else's work if too similar. Architecture and urban art (graffiti, sculptures) only if public domain, not the main subject (photos), and compliant with the country's freedom-of-panorama law; permanent landmarks such as a red London phone booth may appear incidentally, never as the central subject. Prior authorisation is needed for prominent film/TV content, celebrities and performances, branded toys, textiles, jewellery or wallpaper, books and printed media, artworks, posters, maps and technical drawings, packaging, signs and labels. Currency may appear but never flat/scanned-like or in its entirety. A single recognisable product (branded electronics, designer furniture, trademarked jewellery) as the main subject is generally rejected. Vehicles only as secondary subjects.

**Plagiarism:** strictly prohibited; a plagiarised resource is rejected and **the account is permanently banned**. Everything must be original or properly licensed (hold a valid commercial licence for external elements). Collaborative work or shared scenes/models are accepted only with clear differences in composition, framing, lighting, colour, actions and theme. Heavy inspiration that could be confused with the original can also count (the slide uses a Pikachu-style example).

**Repeated submissions:** uploading the same image twice is prohibited whether accepted or rejected; rotated, flipped or reframed copies don't count as original; a vector isn't different if only element positions, colours or sizes change; near-duplicates are declined and re-uploading doesn't raise approval chances.

**Sensitive content (rejected):** inappropriate behaviour, politically offensive or malicious content (e.g. racism), promoting or depicting drug use or violence toward people or animals, animals in circuses, sexually explicit content or nudity, objectifying individuals. Dramatised, subtle, non-realistic depictions of sensitive topics (weapons in neutral contexts, symbolic gestures) are accepted; explicit or realistic violence, suffering, threats or harm is not. Political, historical or cultural content must be neutral; no hate, supremacy, satire or mockery, unrecognised symbols or controversial flags; maps, flags and military content follow internationally recognised standards (dashed lines or subdued tones for disputed territories).

**Artificial downloads:** asking people to download your content for economic reasons, or any method that generates downloads artificially or compensates users for downloading, is forbidden by the terms of use.

## Quick pre-upload checklist

1. Right format and dimensions for the type; preview JPG has the same name.
2. Original, from scratch; no brands, landmarks, franchise-like characters.
3. Vector hand-built (or traced only for traditional-media textures), grouped, English layer names, no embedded bitmaps.
4. Mockup editable area shows a realistic design, not a flat colour.
5. No typos, no outdated dates, no near-duplicates or number-only variations.
6. 5–20 English keywords, no file types or dates; CSV uses semicolons.
7. AI files tagged; no malformed hands, garbled text, or flat textures.
8. Within the 200 files/month limit; test batch of 150–200 in one format for Level 1.
