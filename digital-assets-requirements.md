# Digital assets: platform requirements summary

Compiled October–November 2026 for Quantemp. Use it as context in another session.

**Confidence tags:** **[O]** = platform's own page or deck. **[3P]** = blog, review or news (may be outdated). **[?]** = not found, check the platform.
Magnific/Freepik details come from the official Magnific Contributors deck (read from a screen recording), so they are the most reliable. Royalty percentages are NOT in that deck.

Rule of thumb: honest labelling costs little where AI is accepted (Adobe pays the same royalty for AI content). Where AI is banned, send only fully hand-made work, and keep proof (sketches, layered .ai/.blend files, work-in-progress screenshots).

## 3. Magnific / Freepik official file specs [O]

| Type | Format | Dimensions | Size | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photo | JPG | 2,000–10,000 px; 4–100 MP | 1.5–80 MB | RGB; sRGB, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto RGB or Display P3; lowest compression |
| Vector | EPS (required) | not stated | 0.5–80 MB | RGB |
| Vector preview | JPG, same name as EPS | 2,000–10,000 px | 1.5–80 MB | |
| PSD | Photoshop CC | 1,000–5,000 px; 4–100 MP; match standard paper sizes | 1.5–250 MB | RGB |
| PSD preview | JPG, same name | 2,000–10,000 px | 1.5–80 MB | |

PNG and video are not part of the contributor upload flow [3P].

**Levels and limits:** Level 1 = upload 150–200 assets, all the same format, web upload only. Level 2 = 1–200 per upload; 500 published assets needed for Level 3. Level 3 = 1–200, FTP or web. 200 files per month per account; the upload folder holds 200 at a time. Review takes up to 25 working days. Rejection reasons appear in the file's comments.

**Metadata:** 5–20 English keywords; no repetition, misleading tags, colour lists, file types (EPS10, JPG), dates/numerals unless needed, or special symbols. Describe only what is visible. CSV: semicolon between fields, comma between tags; columns `File name; Title; Keywords; (Prompt); Model`. AI files: use the "Created with artificial intelligence" button or `_ai_generated` in the CSV.

**Standard PSD sizes:** print 300 ppi, screen/web 150 ppi. ISO A3 297×420, A4 210×297, A5 148×210, A6 105×148, DL 99×210 mm. EU business card 85×55 mm, postcard 148×105, A2 poster 420×594, roll-up 850×2,000; US tabloid 11×17 in, legal 8.5×14, letter 8.5×11, half letter 5.5×8.5, US business card 3.5×2, invitation 5×7, RSVP 5×3.5. Banners: leaderboard 728×90 px, medium rectangle 300×250, skyscraper 160×600, square 250×250, half page 300×600.

**PSD naming:** smart-object group `Place your design here (Double click to edit)`, layer inside `Place your design here`; colour layers `Color [item]`; effects `FX [effect]`. Masks on smart objects stay linked.

## 4. Magnific rejection and bad-practice rules [O]

- **Mockups:** editable area must show a realistic design or text. A flat colour or empty placeholder (including a plain green screen) is rejected. Smart objects must work.
- **Templates:** no photos embedded in PSD/EPS (not on any layer, background or smart object). A sample photo may appear only in the JPG preview, marked "IMAGE NOT INCLUDED"; missing label = rejection. Cut-out silhouettes shaped like a person are rejected.
- **Vectors:** Live Trace only for traditional-media textures (watercolour, ink, pencil, charcoal); never for flat art, logos or UI. Use "stacked", group elements, no one big compound path. English layer names. No bitmaps embedded; no dependence on other programs (e.g. vector smart object needing Illustrator). Nothing outside the artboard. A single simple icon must be part of a set.
- **AI files:** reject for malformations (extra fingers), flat/low-detail textures, repetitive images from one prompt, garbled text. AI vectors: individually selectable, no Live Trace/fragments.
- **Photos:** rejected for chromatic aberration, chroma noise, oversaturation or aggressive filters, unjustified or motion blur, over-sharpening (halos), under/overexposure, flat lighting, excessive contrast, upscaling, poor composition. Releases: every recognisable person (minors via parent/guardian), private property, pets (owner). Wild animals fine if not harmed.
- **Typography:** must be coherent with the design; lettering well composed.
- **Dates/devices:** a year in a design must be the current year (next year allowed near year end). Devices ideally no older than 1–2 years. Typos are rejected.
- **Prohibited elements:** signatures, watermarks (also AI/phone), author identifiers, mentions of Magnific, QR codes or links to real sites, people without consent.
- **Copyright:** no logos or brand names; no iconic designs even without logo (Polaroid frames, VW T1 van, LEGO); no landmarks/protected public structures as main or even secondary subject in illustrations; no franchise-like characters (Disney, Pixar, Nickelodeon, Pokémon-like), even modified; CC0 elements from others are not allowed; single branded product as the only subject is generally rejected; currency never flat or whole; vehicles only secondary. Generic up-to-date social media icons are allowed.
- **Plagiarism:** rejected and the account is permanently banned. Reusing others' vector elements (even modified) counts.
- **Repeated submissions:** no same image twice; no rotated/flipped/reframed copies; no position/colour/size-only variants; number-only variants of editable text designs not allowed (non-editable text: about 1–10 versions).
- **Sensitive content:** no explicit violence, drugs, nudity, circus animals, objectification, hate, politically offensive or satirical content; maps and flags follow international standards.
- **Artificial downloads:** forbidden. Suspicious activity can mean suspension without notice, loss of earned income, legal action.
- **Unpublishing:** Published Content, "Unpublish asset"; up to 24 hours to de-index; published files can't be edited (only add releases or unpublish).

## 5. Other platforms: accepted asset types and rules

**Adobe Stock** [O]: photos, vectors, illustrations, video, motion/audio; transparent PNG (real transparency, isolated subject, no checkerboard backgrounds, sRGB, no duplicate PNG+JPEG). Design templates and 3D models by invitation only [3P forum]. JPEG only for photos, 4–100 MP (min 1600×2400), 45 MB. Vectors: AI, EPS or SVG, max 45 MB, artboard 15–65 MP, RGB, offset (0,0), no ZIP, no JPEG previews. Title ~70 characters, first 10 keywords matter most, 15–35 keywords typical. AI label required; also when AI added or changed the main subject; not needed for background extension, object removal or recolouring. Rejections: similar content/spam, quality (messy paths, auto-trace, ungrouped elements), IP, saturated subjects without new angle, logos/brands/packaging.

**Vecteezy** [O/3P]: EPS (Illustrator 10), RGB or CMYK, 3–25 MP (one guide says 4 MP minimum), max 50 MB. Accepts vectors, photos, transparent PNG, high-res video. Refuses logo templates, 3D renderings, JPG illustrations, photos with added text. Free fonts only, no brands/characters, no Live Trace (except scanned textures). No upload quota.

**Dreamstime** [3P]: JPG RGB/sRGB 3–70 MP; AI category required, description must say AI, no AI faces; exclusivity needs 50 approved images.

**Depositphotos** [O/3P]: accepts JPEG photos, vectors, illustrations; no AI from contributors; exam size unconfirmed.

**Shutterstock** [O]: JPEG/TIFF ≥4 MP, sRGB, 50 MB (TIFF 4 GB over FTPS), no PSD/layered TIFF. Vectors EPS 8 or 10, ≥4 MP, up to 100 MB, one artboard, outline live fonts, no photo-traced vectors. Allows one colour + one B&W version of an illustration. 3D renders go as illustrations. No AI.

**Getty/iStock, Alamy, Envato, TurboSquid:** no AI; Alamy vectors EPS only; Envato applications paused; TurboSquid also bans printable weapons and standalone logos, and its CheckMate certification requires selling only there [3P].

**Canva Creators** [O/3P]: Template Creators open (beta, reply can take ~2 months); portfolio of 5–10 pieces at a public no-login link; no follower minimum; review guidelines are shared only after acceptance. Element Creators paused: JPEG, PNG or SVG; elements recreatable inside Canva rejected [3P]; SVG under 3 MB, SVG 1.1; images under 50 MB. Sell Canva Pro-element templates as links, not exports.

**Creative Market** [O/3P]: fonts, graphics, templates, themes, photos, illustrations, icons, mockups, brushes, 3D. Shop requests reviewed by a person. Products built from CM assets can't be sold as customisable templates.

**Creative Fabrica** [O]: graphics, fonts, SVG craft files, embroidery, templates; no licence text or contact details inside files; Single Sales or Subscription licence applies.

**Etsy** [O/3P]: digital files you made or designed; no reselling; AI checkbox + "Designed by" + description. Title 140 characters, 13 tags, up to 5 files of 20 MB each, listing photos ~2,000 px shortest side. Egypt and UAE eligible through Payoneer.

**IconScout** [O]: icons, illustrations, 3D, Lottie, AI images; consistent style per pack; review 7–10 business days. (Iconfinder reference: SVG; detailed 3D PNG min 1024×1024, 2000×2000 recommended; packs of 10–50.)

**Fab (Epic)** [O/3P]: 3D models/assets, engine, exchange and DCC formats, rigged/animated allowed; no visual defects, complete and as described; mature content flagged; tag AI use; tax profile required.

**Superhive** [O]: Blender assets/add-ons, GPL-compatible, no third-party service dependence; fully AI-generated products not allowed; every product reviewed.

**CGTrader** [3P]: original-work warranty; about 1 in 6 uploads AI but ~$1 of every $90 revenue.

## 6. Universal preparation checklist

1. Keep a proof folder per asset (sketches, layered .ai/.psd/.blend, WIP screenshots).
2. Original, from scratch; no brands, logos, landmarks, franchise-like characters, other authors' vectors.
3. Vectors: hand-built, grouped, English layer names, no embedded bitmaps, no auto-trace of flat art, artboard at least 5000×3000 px (passes Adobe 15 MP), RGB, expand strokes/text, no stray anchors.
4. Export both EPS (Illustrator 10) and native .ai/SVG; same-name JPG preview 2,000–10,000 px.
5. Mockups show a realistic design in the editable area; templates contain no embedded photos and use the "IMAGE NOT INCLUDED" label.
6. 5–20 English keywords, 70-character title, no brands/dates/file types.
7. Label AI honestly where accepted; hand-made only for Shutterstock, Getty/iStock, Alamy, Depositphotos, TurboSquid, Envato.
8. Check your AI tool's licence allows commercial resale.
9. No typos, no outdated years, no near-duplicates or number/colour-only variants.
10. Set up Payoneer and PayPal before uploading; check Magnific's 200 files/month limit.
